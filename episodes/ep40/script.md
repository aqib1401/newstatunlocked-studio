SCRIPT:
What if you could hear every thought on Earth? Eight billion minds, all at once, and there's no off switch. Minute one, on the bus. Forty strangers think at the same time. Did I leave the oven on? Is he staring at me? I really need a sandwich. Hour one, school. Your teacher is secretly thinking about lunch, and the smartest girl in class is checking every test answer in her head. Free answers! Then the power spreads. Day two, your whole city. Three million minds, like a stadium that never stops cheering. Normally, your brain filters noise for you. It's why you can follow one friend at a loud party. Scientists call it the cocktail party effect. Your power has no filter. Day five, the whole planet. Eight billion voices... and you can't hear your own. You can't sleep. You can't think. Until one voice says your name, and the roar drops away. Because your brain is tuned to your own name so strongly, it reacts to it even while you're fast asleep. So you follow that voice, across the city, to a shy kid from the back of your class. He thinks your name every day, but he's never said a word to you. I hope they say hi tomorrow. So tomorrow, you sit next to him and say hi. And for the first time in a week, the planet goes quiet. Well... except the guy from the bus. He still really needs a sandwich.
