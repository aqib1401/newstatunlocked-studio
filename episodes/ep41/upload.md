TITLE: What If You Jumped Through Earth?
DESCRIPTION: You jump into a tunnel that goes straight through the planet, from the North Pole to the South Pole.
The fall takes about forty minutes, roughly the same time a satellite skimming the ground needs to fly halfway around the world.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
