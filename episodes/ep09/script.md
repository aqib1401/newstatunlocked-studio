SCRIPT:
What if you could see sounds as colors? Not imagine them. See them. Your alarm goes off, and your whole bedroom floods orange. Your best friend Mia laughs bright green, so you find her across a packed cafeteria instantly. Some people really have this. It's called synesthesia, and their color for each sound stays the same for life. But loud places get blinding. At the fireworks festival, every bang becomes a wall of color, and your little brother Sam lets go of your hand. Through the noise you spot a thin yellow thread, Sam crying at the end of the dock. But from the other dock, a purple scream. A little girl, a stranger, slipping toward the water. In five seconds, the finale turns everything white, and both threads vanish. Five. Four. Three. You only have time to follow one. Your brother's color, or hers. Which one do you follow?
