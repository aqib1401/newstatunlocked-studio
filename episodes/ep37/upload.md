TITLE: What If You Fell Into a Black Hole?
DESCRIPTION: You get two tries at falling into a black hole, and only one of them lets you see the inside.
Near a small black hole, gravity pulls your feet so much harder than your head that you're stretched apart over a thousand kilometers before you reach the edge. Scientists call it spaghettification.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
