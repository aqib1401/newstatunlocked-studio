SCRIPT:
What if you had no fingerprints? Not smudged. Gone. You sneak the last cookie, and when your best friend Mia dusts the jar with cocoa powder, she finds nothing. But then your phone stops unlocking, and the airport scanner can't find you, so you wait three hours. A super rare condition does this, and one doctor nicknamed it immigration delay disease. Your prints formed in the womb, and not even identical twins share them. They're how the world knows you're you. Then, right before your space camp trip with Mia, you knock the science trophy off a shelf and it shatters. Your science teacher Dr. Lee dusts it, and the only prints belong to Mia, who handed it to you. Confess, and you lose the trip. Stay quiet, and Mia does. Dr. Lee gives you five seconds. Five. Four. Three. With no fingerprints, who would ever know it was you?
