TITLE: What If You Could Teleport Anywhere?
DESCRIPTION: You can teleport anywhere on Earth, to the Moon and to the stars, but there's a catch nobody warns you about.
Earth's surface spins east at up to 1,670 km/h, so if you teleported to the far side of the planet you'd keep your old speed and arrive moving backwards at more than 2,000 km/h.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
