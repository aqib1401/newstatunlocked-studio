What if your memory reset every single day? Not just names. Everything. You wake up with a note on your hand, in your own handwriting. You read the wall. It's covered in sticky notes you wrote yourself. Day one, you watch your favorite movie, and every twist still shocks you. Every night, your little brother Sam gives you a piano lesson, and every morning you forget it. Day one hundred, you find Mia crying in the hallway. Every morning, you look at me like a stranger. That night, the pen is in your hand, and sleep is coming. Ten seconds. Nine. Eight. What do you write on your hand?
