TITLE: What If You Were 1,000x Stronger?

DESCRIPTION:
You can hold a bus over your head with one hand, but your day still goes wrong from the first second.
With a thousand times the strength you still couldn't push a car: pushing needs grip, and grip comes from your weight.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
