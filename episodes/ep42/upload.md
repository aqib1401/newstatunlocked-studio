TITLE: What If You Grew 1 Meter Every Day?
DESCRIPTION: You wake up one meter taller every single day: day ten you're eleven meters tall, day thirty you're longer than a blue whale.
Double your height and you get eight times heavier, but your bones only get four times stronger. That's why the biggest animal ever lives in the ocean.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
