What if you shrank to the size of an ant? Not short. Smaller than a raisin. Day one, you trip off the kitchen table and float down like a leaf, without a scratch. Air slows tiny things so much that an ant can survive a fall from any height. Your science teacher, Dr. Lee, starts building a machine to make you big again. Day two, you lift a grain of rice over your head, because your weight shrank faster than your strength. But then something weird starts happening. You're freezing, even in summer, and starving every hour. Here's why. A drop of hot cocoa cools in seconds, while the mug stays hot. Small bodies lose heat fast, which is why a shrew has to eat almost all day to stay warm. Day ten, an old building collapses downtown, and a little girl is trapped behind a crack thinner than a pencil. You squeeze through the dark, find her, and bang on a pipe until the rescuers hear you. They pull her out alive. Then Dr. Lee runs up with the machine. It has one shot, and the charge runs out in ten seconds. But the girl is crying, because her dad is still down there, and only you fit. Ten seconds. Nine. Eight. Your little brother Sam whispers, promise you'll come back big. Seven. Six. Dr. Lee warns that a body this small won't survive the winter. Five. Four. Your brother, or her dad. Would you stay this tiny?
