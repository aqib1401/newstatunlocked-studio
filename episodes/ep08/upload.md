TITLE: What If You Could Never Forget Anything?

DESCRIPTION:
What if you remembered every second of your life, forever?
Hyperthymesia is real: a handful of people can recall almost every day of their lives, and while you sleep your brain trims away most of the day's details on purpose.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
