What if you could control every dream? Every single night. The second you fall asleep, you know it's a dream, so you fly to the moon in your pyjamas. It's called lucid dreaming, and about half of people have had at least one. Small studies suggest practising in one can help a little, so you practise piano all night, and by Friday your teacher notices. There's just one rule. If anything wakes you up, that dream is gone forever. Tonight, your grandpa is in it. He passed away last spring, and he's finally about to tell you what he never got to say. Then, back in your bedroom, your phone rings. It's your best friend Mia, and she never calls this late. The dream starts to shake. Five. Four. Three. Wake up, and Grandpa is gone. Hold on, and Mia gets no answer. Grandpa, or Mia? Who do you choose?
