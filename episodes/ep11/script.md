SCRIPT:
What if you could see heat? Not feel it. See it. On the bus, you can see which seat someone just left. Your cat goes missing, and you find her in one second, a little orange blob in the dark garage. Pit vipers really do this, sensing heat to hunt in total darkness, and firefighters use heat cameras to see through smoke. But anything really hot glares at you like the sun. Then one night, your apartment building catches fire. Smoke fills the hallway. Nobody can see, except you. Through the gray you spot your best friend Mia, who was sleeping over, passed out by the stairs. And at the other end, under his bed, your little brother Sam, curled up and hiding. The flames are getting brighter, and in three seconds you'll be blind. Three. Two. One. You only reach one. Mia, or Sam. Who do you save?
