SCRIPT:
What if you remembered being a baby? Not a blurry picture. Everything. When you were one, you threw your dad's wedding ring behind the radiator, so twelve years later you walk him straight to it, and he almost cries. Normally that's impossible. Almost nobody remembers anything before age three, because a baby's brain is still building its memory filing system. But you remember everything, even the day you got the little scar on your chin. Your dad has blamed himself ever since. He thinks he left the stair gate open. He didn't. Your grandma was babysitting, and she looked away for one second. And Grandma still looks after your little brother Sam every single day. Tonight, Dad finally asks, Do you remember how it happened? Five. Four. Three. Tell him, and Grandma might lose Sam. Stay quiet, and Dad carries it forever. Would you tell him?
