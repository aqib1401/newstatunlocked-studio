SCRIPT:
What if you could hear your eyes move? Not a blink. Every glance. You look left, and something inside your skull goes shhk. Your heartbeat booms, and chewing crisps sounds like an avalanche. But rest your head on your desk, and you hear a pencil tapping three rows away, through the wood. It's real: a tiny hole in the bone by your inner ear lets sound travel through your body into your hearing. Some people with it can even hear a tuning fork touching their ankle. Then comes the exam that decides your scholarship. In the silent hall, you hear it through the floor. Two rows back, your best friend Mia's heart is racing. She's panicking. One word, and you're both disqualified. Raise your hand, and nobody believes a sound only you can hear. Five... Four... Three... Your scholarship, or your best friend. Who do you listen to?
