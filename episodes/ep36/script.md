SCRIPT:
What if your brain ran a thousand times faster? Every second of your life would feel like seventeen minutes. Your teacher opens her mouth to say one word, and in that gap, you plan your whole weekend, minute by minute. A football flies at your face, but to you it drifts in like a soap bubble. Even a bullet would float past slower than you walk. But there's a catch, and it's hiding in your own body. Your brain is fast. Your body and the world are not. Your muscles still move at normal speed, so lifting your arm takes eight long minutes. One blink is five minutes of darkness. When your best friend Mia says hello, that one word takes five minutes to reach you. And to everyone watching, you look frozen, just staring, while inside your head you've lived a whole afternoon. Here's the weird part. Your brain is only two percent of your body, but it already burns a fifth of all your energy, just by thinking. So you stop talking, and start thinking. A ten minute bus ride lasts a week inside your head. You design a whole city in your mind, down to every window, before the bus even reaches school. In one year, you live through a thousand. But you live them alone, in a world of statues, waiting nearly an hour for one goodnight hug to end. One night, you find out you can switch it off. Just once, and forever. Keep it, and you could solve problems no human ever could, but you'll spend a thousand years watching Mia grow up, one slow blink at a time. Switch it off, and you're ordinary again, back at the same speed as everyone you love. Which one do you choose... and why?
