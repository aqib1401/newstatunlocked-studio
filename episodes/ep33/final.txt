What if you could freeze time? One snap of your fingers, and the whole world stops dead... except you. But every second you steal gets paid for, and the bill goes to you. Here's the catch the movies skip. If time truly stopped, light would stop too, and the world would go pitch black. So your power cheats a little. Light still reaches your eyes, and anything within an arm's length of you keeps moving. Everything else turns into a statue. Snap. Your phone hangs in midair, an inch above the floor, so you just pluck it out of the air. You throw a ball, and the moment it leaves your reach, it freezes in place. Outside, it's pouring, and every raindrop hangs in the air like a glass bead. Each drop you pass falls to the ground, so behind you, a dry tunnel cuts straight through the rain. A hummingbird floats beside a flower, its wings, which beat dozens of times a second, stuck mid flap. Then you find a lightning bolt, frozen across the sky. It's about five times hotter than the surface of the Sun, so you don't get too close. And you get bolder. You freeze time for a whole week and read every book in the library. You freeze it for a month and walk across an entire country, past cars stuck on the highway and planes parked in the clouds. But your bubble keeps one clock running... yours. Your heart beats about a hundred thousand times a day, frozen world or not. Your hair grows. Your shoes stop fitting. Your best friend Mia is still eleven, and you're fifteen. Then twenty. You've lived whole secret years that nobody else ever saw, and you can't tell anyone where they went. And here's the strangest part. If someone else had this power and froze the world right now, you'd never know. You'd be a statue too, and every clock on Earth would agree that nothing happened. So maybe someone just did. Snap.
