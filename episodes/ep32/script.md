SCRIPT:
What if the Earth stopped spinning? Not slowly. All at once. Right now, even sitting still, you're moving. At the equator, the ground under your feet spins at almost seventeen hundred kilometers an hour. You just can't feel it, because the air, the oceans and you are all moving with it. So when the planet slams on the brakes... nothing else does. The air keeps going. Winds faster than any hurricane ever recorded sweep across the planet in seconds. Cars, trees, even whole houses tumble east. The oceans keep going too, and giant waves roll over the coasts. But if you happened to be standing on the North Pole, you'd barely notice. Up there, the ground was hardly moving to begin with. So you'd watch the sky instead, because the sun stops moving. One side of the Earth gets six months of daylight. The other side freezes in six months of night. And slowly, something even stranger happens. Earth is a little fat around the middle, because spinning pushes the oceans outward. Without the spin, all that water drains toward the poles. Two giant oceans form at the top and bottom, and one huge continent rises around the middle of the world. A brand new map. So when the Earth stops... where do you want to be standing?
