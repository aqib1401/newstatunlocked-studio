What if you could smell ten thousand times better? Dog level. Your friend Leo swears he didn't eat your fries, but you smell ketchup on his breath. You lose your phone, and sniff it out from under the couch. But then you stop sleeping. The trash outside smells like a monster, your brother's socks are a crime scene, and your head pounds all day, because your nose has no off switch. So you sleep in nose plugs. Somewhere on your street, there's a gas leak, and everyone is asleep. To track it down, you have to take the full hit. You pull the plugs out anyway, and follow it straight to your neighbor's door. Would you smell ten thousand times better, if it cost you your sleep? Yes or no?
