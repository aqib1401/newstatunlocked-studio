TITLE: What If Your Reaction Time Was 10x Faster?
LENGTH: 60 s | WORDS: 198 | VOICE: kokoro am_michael 1.1

SCRIPT:
What if your reaction time was ten times faster? Not quick. Ten times. Your friend knocks his coffee off the table, and your hand is already under the cup before it even starts to fall. A fly buzzes past, and you just pinch it out of the air. Most people need about a quarter of a second to react to anything. You need two and a half hundredths. But then something weird starts happening. Your best friend Leo throws you a fastball, and it floats toward you like a balloon. You swing anyway. And you miss. Because here's the catch: only your brain got faster. Your arms are as slow as ever. You see everything coming, and you still can't get there in time. At races, you have to hold yourself back, because if you react in under a tenth of a second off the gun, they decide you must have guessed. Then, at the school final, you see your little brother slip off the railing. Your brain sees it instantly. Your legs don't. The gold medal, or your brother? You run. You catch him. You finish dead last. Would you still want this power? Yes or no?

FACTS USED:
1. Average human reaction time is about 0.25 s: high
2. A reaction under 0.1 s after the starting gun counts as a false start: high
