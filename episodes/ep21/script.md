What if your body glowed in the dark? Here's the weird part: it already does. Scientists in Japan filmed it, about a thousand times too faint for your eyes to see. Now turn it up. Lights off, and you shine like a candle. When the school loses power, you lead your whole class down the dark stairs, and everyone cheers. But there's a catch. Like a candle, you only light a few steps around you. Then, on a night hike with your little brother Sam and your best friend Leo, a storm hits and every phone dies. Sam is eight, lost in the trees, crying. Leo slipped into a ditch by the river, ankle twisted, and the water is rising. Both are shouting your name from opposite sides, and you can only light one path. Five. Four. Three. Your little brother, or your best friend. Who do you glow for?
