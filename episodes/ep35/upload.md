TITLE: What If the Sun Vanished?
DESCRIPTION: The Sun disappears right now, and for eight whole minutes nobody on Earth can tell.
Sunlight and even gravity take about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach us, and Jupiter would keep shining for over an hour after the Sun was gone.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
