SCRIPT:
What if you never blinked again? Not once. Not ever. You win every staring contest, and your friend Leo gives up in four seconds. You even stare down the class goldfish, and it's a tie. But soon, everything goes blurry, and your eyes feel full of sand. Because every blink was doing a secret job. Each one wipes fresh tears across your eyes, like a windshield wiper. The clear front of your eye has no blood vessels. It gets its oxygen from the air, through that thin layer of tears. Without blinking, that layer dries up, and it stings. So every few minutes, you hold your eyelids shut with your fingers, just to see again. That night is your little brother Sam's school play. Your eyes are burning, but if you close them now, you'll miss his one line. You keep them open. Would you give up blinking forever? Yes or no?
