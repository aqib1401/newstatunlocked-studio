TITLE: What If You Never Felt Hungry Again?

DESCRIPTION:
What if you never felt hungry again, not even a little?
Hunger is an alarm: a hormone called ghrelin, made mostly in your stomach, tells your brain it's time to eat. Without it, your blood sugar can drop so low that you pass out.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
