TITLE: What If Your Heart Beat Only Six Times a Minute?

DESCRIPTION:
What if your heart beat only six times a minute, like a diving whale?
When seals and whales dive, their hearts slow down on purpose to save oxygen. A blue whale's can drop to about two beats a minute.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
