TITLE: What If You Grew 10x Taller?

DESCRIPTION:
What if you were ten times taller? Seventeen meters, taller than a five-story building.
Grow ten times taller and you get about a thousand times heavier, but your bones only get about a hundred times stronger. That's the square-cube law, and it's why elephants have legs like tree trunks.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
