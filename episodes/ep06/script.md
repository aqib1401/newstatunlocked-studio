SCRIPT:
What if you were ten times taller? Not basketball tall. Seventeen meters, taller than a five-story building. Day one, you step over a school bus on your way to class. Not onto it. Over it. Then on day five, an apartment block catches fire. You kneel beside it and lift twelve people out of the windows, one by one. The whole street chants for you. But that night, your knees throb, and every step feels like carrying a truck. Because here's what nobody tells you. Grow ten times taller, and you get about a thousand times heavier, but your bones only get about a hundred times stronger. That's why elephants walk on legs like tree trunks. The tallest man in history wasn't even three meters tall, and he still needed leg braces to walk. You're six times taller than him. Your doctor studies your x-ray and goes pale. One more kneel, and your knees will snap. You limp for four days. On day nine, the old river bridge cracks in half. Two cars slide off opposite ends into the water. To reach either one, you have to kneel. In one car, your best friend Mia, who never laughed at you. In the other, a little girl, alone, banging on the window. Mia yells, Don't leave me! Ten seconds. Nine. Eight. Both cars are filling with water. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Your best friend, or a stranger's little girl. Ten times taller, and one kneel left. Who do you save?
