TITLE: What If You Were Allergic to Water?

DESCRIPTION:
What if one raindrop gave you hives, and your little brother just fell into the deep end?
Water allergy is real: it's called aquagenic urticaria, and it's so rare most doctors never see a case. Itchy hives appear within minutes of water touching the skin.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
