SCRIPT:
What if you were allergic to water? Not a little itchy. Water itself. One raindrop lands on your arm, and minutes later it bursts into red, itchy bumps. So you carry an umbrella, and a doctor's note gets you out of swim class forever. But for some people, even their own sweat or tears set it off. It's real, and so rare that most doctors never see a single case. Then your family drags you to a pool party, so you sit in the shade, bone dry. That's why you're the only one who sees your little brother Sam slip into the deep end. His hand goes under. The lifeguard is looking the other way, and the music is loud. Jump in, and you're covered in burning hives for the next hour. Or scream, and hope someone hears. Five... Four... Three... Would you jump in for him? Yes or no?
