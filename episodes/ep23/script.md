SCRIPT:
What if your skin couldn't be cut? Not tough. Unbreakable. You fall on broken glass without a scratch, so you hug a cactus for a dare. When a bus crashes, you climb through the jagged metal to pull four kids out. Soon firefighters are calling you every week. But at your checkup, the nurse's needle bends in half. Your shield keeps out help too. Then your little brother Sam gets sick. He needs new bone marrow, the soft stuff inside bones that makes blood. A brother or sister only matches about one in four times, and you match. But doctors take it from your hip bone with a needle, and a needle only gets in if you give up your power, forever. The nurse lifts the needle. Five. Four. Three. Sam, or every stranger you'd pull from the next wreck. Would you let your skin be cut again?
