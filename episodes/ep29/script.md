What if you had perfect pitch? Not good at music. Perfect. The microwave beeps, and you say, that's a B flat. At the school concert, you tune the band's guitar by ear seconds before the curtain opens, and the whole show is saved. Only about one person in ten thousand can name any note they hear. But now every wrong note hurts. A singer on the radio is a tiny bit flat, and to you it sounds like nails on a chalkboard. So your music teacher makes you the judge of the talent show final. Your best friend Mia sings her heart out, and everyone cheers, but only you hear her miss every high note. Then a shy new kid sings every note exactly right. The winner gets a place at music school. Five... Four... Three... Your best friend, or the kid who earned it? Who wins?
