TITLE: What If You Had Perfect Pitch?

DESCRIPTION:
What if you could name every note you hear, and every wrong one hurt?
Only about one person in ten thousand has perfect pitch: they can name a note, like a microwave beep, without hearing any reference first.
Some parts are fiction. The science around them is real.
